GenoLigand integrates open-source scientific engines through GenoLigand-owned adapters. The canonical schemas, scoring, evidence ledger, report semantics and user-facing interpretation remain in GenoLigand.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| RDKit | Cheminformatics engine | BSD-style upstream license | https://www.rdkit.org/ |
| Open Babel | Molecular format conversion | GPL-compatible upstream license terms | https://openbabel.org/ |
| MDTraj | Trajectory feature extraction | LGPL/BSD-style upstream notices as distributed | https://www.mdtraj.org/ |
| MDAnalysis | Trajectory analysis | GPL-compatible upstream license terms | https://www.mdanalysis.org/ |
| PDBFixer | Structure preparation | MIT-style upstream license | https://github.com/openmm/pdbfixer |
